## What to Look for in Modern Air Fryers

When evaluating air fryers and connected kitchen appliances, do not get distracted by flashy LED screens alone. Focus on airflow dynamics and thermal control.

*   **CFM (Cubic Feet per Minute) Airflow:** Look for appliances that emphasize high-velocity circulation. Faster airflow renders fat quicker before the exterior of the protein overcooks.
*   **Integrated Thermal Probes:** Smart fryers equipped with leave-in temperature sensors read the internal meat temperature in real time, auto-shutting off the moment target lethality or doneness is reached.
*   **Perforated Drop Trays:** Ensure the interior basket features deep-ridged oil channels so rendered fat stays separated from the food and below the airflow vortex.

## 3 Common Mistakes That Waste Your Calories and Tech

1.  **Overcrowding the Basket:** Stacking chicken or veggies stops air circulation. Instead of rendering fat, you steam the food, trapping oil in soggy breading or skin. Cook in a single layer.
2.  **Adding Oil to Naturally Fatty Proteins:** Bacon, chicken wings, and salmon do not need oil sprays. The appliance is designed to draw fat *out*, not cook fat *in*. Save oil sprays for dry starches like sweet potato fries.
3.  **Ignoring the Bottom Reservoir:** Leaving rendered fat in the pan across multiple cooking cycles risks reaching the oil's smoke point, causing bitter smoke to recirculate back into your fresh meal. Clean the tray after every use.
