# The Numbers Don't Lie: CeraVe Moisturising Cream — 2026 Buyer's Guide

*Published 2026-09-13*

At 340g, the big tub is **6.8 times larger** than a 50g tube — yet the per-gram price on Amazon India can swing so wide that the largest size sometimes works out far cheaper than the smallest. Size choice is part of the smart purchase, not an afterthought.

## What to Look For

- **Weight, not just price.** Compare price ÷ grams. The 50g, 170g and 340g options are the same cream, but the price per gram usually drops as the pack grows.
- **Seller and fulfilment.** Prefer Amazon-fulfilled listings or sellers with easy returns. Grey imports are common on Amazon India, and they can have shorter remaining shelf life.
- **Import/origin.** US, UK, EU and India-stock versions all show up. The core formula is essentially the same; don’t overpay for a “version” unless you have a specific preference.
- **Seal and batch code.** Genuine tubs have a safety seal under the lid. Ask the seller to confirm a printed batch code if the product photos don’t show one.

## Top Features That Matter

- **Three ceramides** — ceramide NP, AP and EOP are the barrier-repair ingredients this product is known for.
- **MVE delivery** — releases ingredients gradually, which is why one thin layer can last several hours.
- **Hyaluronic acid + niacinamide** — these add hydration and soothing, while the cream’s petrolatum and dimethicone lock moisture in.
- **Fragrance-free and non-comedogenic** — good for sensitive and dry skin, but not automatically ideal for oily, breakout-prone skin.
- **Thick balm texture** — this is a true cream, not a lotion. It suits dry to very dry skin, especially on the body, hands, feet, and winter face use.

## Common Mistakes

- **Buying purely by pack size.** A cheap 340g tub is no deal if it arrives with an expiry date three months away. Always check “best before” in the product Q&A or seller images.
- **Using too much.** A pea-sized amount is enough for the face; a nickel-size amount is enough for one limb. Excess product just sits on the skin.
- **Treating it like a lotion.** It needs warmth. Rub it between your palms and press onto slightly damp skin for better absorption.
- **Ignoring skin type.** If you’re oily or acne-prone, this cream’s heavy emollients may feel suffocating or clog pores. Use it only on dry areas, or switch to CeraVe lotion.
- **Expecting a treatment.** It repairs the moisture barrier and soothes dryness, but it is not an acne medicine, eczema cure, or anti-ager.

## Who Each Tier Suits

- **Budget — the 340g tub.** Suits families, people with very dry skin, or anyone using it on both face and body. You get the lowest cost per gram, but you must commit to finishing it before expiry.
- **Mid — the 50g or 170g tube/tub.** Suits individuals, travellers, or those who only need a facial moisturiser. It’s more expensive per gram but reduces waste and is easier to carry.
- **Premium — an Amazon-fulfilled listing with fast delivery, easy returns, and seller-backed authenticity.** Suits first-time buyers or anyone nervous about grey imports. You pay extra for peace of mind and a smoother refund process — not for a better formula.
